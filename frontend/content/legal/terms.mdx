## 5. Data and compliance signals — important limits

The Service returns **informational signals, not regulated advice**:

- Sanctions screening is performed at the **bank level** (BIC8). It does **not** screen beneficiary or account-holder names. It is **not** a regulated AML/CFT product and does not fulfil name-screening obligations under AML law. Use a regulated provider for name-level KYC/AML.
- Reference data (BIC directories, SEPA registers, Swiss BankMaster, sanctions lists) is compiled from third-party sources and refreshed on the schedule shown in the [documentation](/docs). Data may contain errors or lag behind reality.
