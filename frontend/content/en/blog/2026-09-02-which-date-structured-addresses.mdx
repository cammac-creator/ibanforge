There is no single worldwide date for the end of unstructured payment addresses. When preparing accounting software or an ERP migration, distinguish the data format, the payment network’s timetable and what the receiving bank accepts.

> **Sources reviewed on 4 November 2026.** On 29 October, EPC postponed setting its replacement deadline: its board will meet by February 2027 to decide it. **February 2027 is not the retirement date for unstructured addresses.** [EPC decision](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/news-insights/news/epc-defers-setting-new-end-date-unstructured-address-format-until-february-2027).

## The timetable by payment network

| Network | Operator’s position in the reviewed source | Primary source |
|---|---|---|
| SIX, Swiss Payment Standards | SPS 2026 remains scheduled for **14 November 2026**. An address provided must be structured or hybrid. SIC does not validate addresses; banks may offer additional options outside SPS. | [Swiss Payments](https://www.six-group.com/en/products-services/banking-services/payment-standardization/swiss-payments.html) |
| EPC, all five payment schemes | Retirement planned for November 2026 postponed. **Decision on a replacement date by February 2027**, with no retirement date announced in that decision. | [Decision of 29 October 2026](https://www.europeanpaymentscouncil.eu/news-insights/news/epc-defers-setting-new-end-date-unstructured-address-format-until-february-2027) |
| Swift, CBPR+ payments | Payments changes deferred; address timetable under consultation, with an update promised **by December 2026 at the latest**. | [27 August announcement, updated on 21 September](https://www.swift.com/insights/news/swift-accepts-community-request-extend-structured-address-migration-iso-20022-payment-messages) |
| Eurosystem T2 | TARGET releases moved to **28 November 2026**. A temporary measure continues to allow fully unstructured addresses in T2 RTGS messages. | [ECB, 4 September 2026](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/press/intro/news/html/ecb.mipnews260904.en.html) |
| Fedwire Funds Service | Release moved to **November 2027**; the reviewed FAQ does not yet announce the exact day. | [November 2027 release FAQ](https://www.frbservices.org/resources/financial-services/wires/fedwire-services-frequently-asked-questions/november-2026-release-frequently-asked-questions/) |
| Bank of England, CHAPS and RTGS | November 2026 release deferred in full; incorporating the changes into **November 2027 is expected, subject to confirmation**. | [ISO 20022 page, updated on 27 August 2026](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/payments/rtgs-renewal-programme/iso-20022) |

A release date, an expected announcement and the retirement date of a format are different things. One operator’s postponement does not automatically change another’s timetable.

## What February 2027 means for EPC

The October announcement supersedes the expectation of an October decision in the previous version of this page. Structured and hybrid formats are already supported; EPC recommends continuing migration, preferably to fully structured addresses. The delay is therefore no reason to stop preparing the data.

The earlier EPC dates of 22 and then 15 November 2026 are no longer current deadlines. Do not simply replace them with “February 2027” in a migration plan.

## 14 November remains a separate Swiss milestone

SIX maintains SPS 2026 and QR-bill guidelines 2.4 for 14 November. Under SPS, town and country must be populated whenever an address is provided. **This does not mean that SIC will automatically reject every unstructured payment**: SIX says address validation will be waived, and banks may retain options outside SPS.

QR-bills have their own rule: only structured address type S has been permitted since **22 November 2025**. That earlier change is separate from the payment-order timetable.

## Do not confuse the Swift and T2 dates

Swift’s 21 September update schedules a deferred release for 12 June 2027, covering the message categories it lists. It does not present that day as the new CBPR+ address deadline: the address section still describes consultation and an update by December at the latest.

For T2, 28 November is the deployment date announced by the ECB. The same announcement provides temporary support for unstructured addresses without giving its end date. Do not present 28 November as their automatic rejection date.

## Prepare supplier data without waiting for a single deadline

1. Identify the payment networks actually used and the requirements of the bank receiving the files.
2. Preserve the original data, then separate town and country in a working copy. Ask the supplier to confirm ambiguous addresses.
3. Test files with the bank and retain its response: a format check does not prove that a payment will be accepted.

The [address-check documentation](/docs/structured-addresses) explains the format rules applied by `POST /v1/address/check`. Each finding cites its document; the result does not determine when a bank applies it. The [QR-bill checker](/tools/qr-bill) checks a complete bill, and the [file audit](/audit) helps prepare a review of supplier data. None of these checks confirms account ownership or authorises a payment.
